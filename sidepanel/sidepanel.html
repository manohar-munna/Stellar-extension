What you'll see here

Every step of the agent is shown as it happens:

  1. Capture the visible tab and tag every clickable element ([BUTTON_03]).
  2. Detect credentials & personal data — DOM rules on-device, plus the Gemini vision detector.
  3. Redact each hit into a semantic tag like [EMAIL_01].
  4. Send only the sanitized frame — with a leak check of the outbound payload.
  5. Reason: Gemini proposes one action.
  6. Validate locally (allow-list, risky-action approval, tag resolution) and Execute.

Each task gets its own tab. Every step shows a short brief — what was hidden, what was sent and what the model saw. Click a step (or a segment of its stage bar) for the full detail.
